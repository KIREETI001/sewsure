# Sewing clubs and makers' groups

Draft for [FOUNDER_NAME] to edit and send from their own account. Sewing clubs are often run by volunteers. Ask first, and offer to come along or to speak for a few minutes rather than posting into a group.

## Message to a club organiser

Subject: Final-year project: a short talk or a chat with members?

Hello [GROUP_NAME] organisers,

I'm [FOUNDER_NAME], a final-year student at NTU. My team is building SewSure, which helps people think through redesigning a garment they already own before they cut any fabric. It is still in development and not available yet.

Would you be open to a 5-minute introduction at a meeting, or to a short chat with a few members who might be interested? I'd like to learn how members decide whether to alter a piece or make something new, and what would make a checklist or cut guide useful. We aren't selling anything.

If it isn't a good fit, that's completely fine. Thank you for reading this.

Best,
[FOUNDER_NAME]

## Short talk outline (for a meeting, 5 minutes)

1. Who we are, in one sentence.
2. The problem: clothes that could be changed but aren't.
3. What we are testing. Be clear that the tool is not finished.
4. What we would like from members: a short first chat of about 15 minutes, if they want one.
5. How to contact us, and how to say no.

Do not show a demo of features that are not built yet. Label anything planned as planned.

## Notes for the founder

- Bring printed or written notes only if the organiser agrees.
- Do not collect names or emails at the meeting unless each person opts in on the spot. Log the count, not the names.
- Record the visit with the channel and the date, and the number of people reached. Use code `P` only for individual first chats, and never write down names.
