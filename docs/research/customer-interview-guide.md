# Customer discovery interview guide (30 minutes)

For: founders. Participant code: `P__`. Date: ____ Founder: ____ Note-taker: ____

**Goal:** understand what people do today with clothes they no longer wear, what stops them, and what they would pay for. We are learning, not selling. Do not demo the product in this session. Do not mention the earlier survey.

**Rules for the interviewer**
- Ask about past behaviour ("tell me about the last time"), not hypotheticals, wherever possible.
- Stay silent after an answer for 3 seconds. Follow up with "Tell me more" or "What happened next?".
- Never suggest an answer. Never use words like "fit issues" or "AI" until the participant does, except in section 6 where AI is the topic.
- Confirm consent is recorded and ask about audio before starting. Pause recording on request.

Each question has a note slot. Write what they said, in their words. Mark quotes with "".

## 0. Opening (2 min)

Ask: "Are you 18 or over?" (if no, thank and end). Read: "Thanks for your time. I'm learning how people deal with clothes in their wardrobe. There are no right answers and I'm not testing you. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Is it still OK to take notes? Is it OK to record audio (optional)?"

- Consent confirmed in tracker: ☐   Audio: ☐ yes ☐ no

## 1. Unworn clothes (5 min)

1.1 Think of clothes in your wardrobe you rarely or never wear. Tell me about a couple of them.
> Notes:

1.2 What happened with the last item like that: how did it end up unworn?
> Notes:

1.3 What have you done with items like that in the past?
> Notes:

1.4 How do you feel when you look at those items?
> Notes:

## 2. Fit problems (5 min)

2.1 Tell me about the last time clothing didn't work for you. What was the situation?
> Notes:

2.2 What did you do about it?
> Notes:

2.3 Have you bought or been given second-hand clothes? Tell me about that experience.
> Notes:

2.4 (If relevant) Tell me about a time you decided not to keep or buy something. What led to that?
> Notes:

## 3. Changing clothes and fear of ruining them (5 min)

3.1 Have you ever changed or repaired a piece of clothing, or thought about it? Tell me about it.
> Notes:

3.2 Tell me how that went from idea to what you ended up doing. (If nothing happened: what did you do instead?)
> Notes:

3.3 Have you ever decided not to do something to a garment? Tell me about it.
> Notes:

3.4 How did you decide? What did you take into account?
> Notes:

## 4. Sewing skill and tools (4 min)

4.1 How would you describe your own sewing experience? What have you actually made or fixed?
> Notes:

4.2 What do you have at home that you'd use for this (machine, needles, scissors, measuring tape, ruler, printer)?
> Notes:

4.3 Where do you learn things like this today?
> Notes:

4.4 How much time could you imagine spending on a project like this? (Ask for a specific recent comparison.)
> Notes:

## 5. Tailors and paying for help (4 min)

5.1 Have you used a tailor or alteration service? Tell me about the last time.
> Notes:

5.2 How did you find them? How did you decide?
> Notes:

5.3 What was easy or hard about explaining what you wanted?
> Notes:

5.4 What would make you pay someone to do a job on a garment, rather than do it yourself or leave it? Where is the limit?
> Notes:

## 6. Trust in instructions and willingness to pay (4 min)

Follow-up only if the participant raises computer- or AI-made guides themselves: "Tell me more about that." Then ask: "Have you ever used something like that? What happened?"

Read: "Now some questions about guides and instructions you have used. Think of real examples."

6.1 Tell me about a time you followed instructions or a guide you weren't sure about. How did you decide whether to trust it?
> Notes:

6.2 What makes you trust or doubt a source of instructions, such as a person, video or app?
> Notes:

6.3 Have you used any app or website to get instructions for making or changing things? What was it like?
> Notes:

6.4 Have you paid for any app, pattern, tutorial or kit for hobbies or clothing? Tell me about the last one: what, and how much?
> Notes:

6.5 Tell me what you have paid for in the past for hobbies or clothing, and what made it worth it (or not). Stop there. Do not suggest a price or pricing model; if they name one, record it in their words.
> Notes (record exact figures and currency):

## 7. Close (1 min)

7.1 Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
> Notes:

7.2 Would you be open to us contacting you once about trying an early version? (Yes/No, recorded in tracker only.)
> Notes:

Read: "Thank you. Notes will be de-identified. You can ask us to delete your data any time using the contact on the consent form."
