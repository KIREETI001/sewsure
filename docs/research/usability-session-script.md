# Usability session script (observed tasks)

For: founder (facilitator) plus one note-taker where possible. Duration: 40 minutes. Participant code: `P__`. Build/version: ____ Device: ____ Date: ____

Prerequisites: consent recorded (`consent-form-draft.md`, after H-05). Use a **synthetic or the participant's own garment with consent** and **made-up measurements** unless the participant has agreed otherwise. Do not store real photos or real body measurements in the repo.

You are testing the app, not the participant. Say so. Previews are approximate: do not describe results as safe, exact or guaranteed.

## Facilitator rules

- Think-aloud: ask them to say what they are looking at and thinking.
- Do not help unless they are stuck for over 60 seconds or ask. Prompts, in order: (1) "What do you think you could do here?" (2) "Look at the bottom of the screen." (3) Show them. Log the level of help used.
- Never explain a screen before they interpret it.
- Stop a task if frustration is high. Mark as not completed.

## Warm-up (3 min)

Tell me about a garment you'd like to change. (Note intent; no measuring here.)

## Tasks

Give each task as a scenario on a card. Start the timer when they begin, stop when they say they're done or you stop them.

| # | Task | Scenario to read | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Capture | "You have this garment and want to try a new look. Add it to the app." | Garment photo captured/uploaded; intention stated; any category/photo quality prompts handled |
| T2 | Measure | "The app will ask for some measurements. Enter them." (use provided made-up values) | Measurements entered and confirmed; understands units |
| T3 | Review results | "The app has suggested some designs. Tell me what you see and which, if any, you'd choose." | Opens a design; can say what status it has and what it means; does not assume exactly three options exist |
| T4 | Read the cut guide | "You've chosen a design. Find what you would cut." | Finds the cut guide; can state which part is to be cut and where it is approximate |
| T5 | Ruler checkpoint | "The app asks you to check the scale with a ruler. Do that." | Places ruler as instructed; completes checkpoint; understands what it means |
| T6 | (Optional) Tailor path | "You don't want to do this yourself. What would you do?" | Finds the tailor brief; says what would be sent |

- *Error* = wrong tap, wrong entry, wrong interpretation, or an app failure (mark which).
- *Confidence* asked after each task: "How confident are you that you did that right? 1 = not at all, 5 = completely."
- Record the reading of any status or warning in their own words.

## Comprehension probes (ask after T3 and T4)

- "What does this label mean to you?" (state badge)
- "Would you cut your garment now? What would make you more or less sure?"
- "What is approximate here and what isn't?"

Record any belief that the preview is exact; this is a critical finding.

## Close (5 min)

1. What was the hardest part?
2. What was clearest?
3. What would you want to know before cutting your own clothes?
4. Single Ease Question for the whole journey: "Overall, how easy or difficult was it? 1 very difficult – 7 very easy."

## Severity rating (used by D7 in tagging)

- **Critical:** participant believes an unverified or approximate result is safe to cut; or a data-loss/blocked step.
- **Major:** task failed or needed level 3 help.
- **Minor:** slowed, recovered unaided.
- **Note:** preference or idea.

## Summary format (in insight template)

Report per task: completed unaided *x of n* participants, median time, total errors, help levels, median confidence. Report n each time. Do not generalise from small samples.
