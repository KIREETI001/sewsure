# Recruiting screener (Google Forms)

Draft by D7 for founders to build as a Google Form. Do not include names or contact details in this repo. Collect them only in the Form's own response sheet and the founders' private tracker.

**Form description (top of form):**
"We're students researching how people deal with clothes they no longer wear. A 30-minute chat, no preparation. Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any time. Answers here are used only to choose who to invite. We will not share them."

**Form settings:** collect no email automatically; do not require sign-in; add a consent checkbox (Q0).

## Questions

**Q0. (required checkbox)** I agree the team may use my answers to decide whether to invite me and to contact me about a chat. ☐

**Q1. Which best describes you?** (single choice)
- Wearing mostly clothes I bought new
- Often wear second-hand or hand-me-downs
- I alter, repair or make clothes
- I work as a tailor or alteration professional
- Other: ____

*Routing: tailors/alterers go to the tailor section (Q9–Q12). Everyone else continues.*

**Q2. How would you describe your sewing experience?** (single choice)
- None
- Beginner (a few repairs, e.g. buttons)
- Intermediate (can follow a pattern)
- Experienced

**Q3. In the last 12 months, have you had clothes that you stopped wearing or did not buy because of how they fit?** (Yes / No / Not sure)

**Q4. How many items in your wardrobe do you rarely or never wear?** (0 / 1–5 / 6–15 / More than 15)

**Q5. In the last 12 months, have you used a tailor or alteration service?** (Yes / No)

**Q6. Which tools do you have at home?** (multi-select: sewing machine, needle and thread, fabric scissors, measuring tape, ruler, printer, none of these)

**Q7. What phone do you use?** (Android / iPhone / Other) *(for later app testing; not for selection now)*

**Q8. Are you aged 18 or over?** (Yes / No) *(No → thank and end form)*

**Tailor section**

**Q9.** What kind of business? (own shop / works for a shop / home-based / other)

**Q10.** About how many alteration or redesign jobs per week? (<5 / 5–15 / 16–30 / >30)

**Q11.** How do customers usually request work? (walk-in / phone / messaging app / referral / online / other; multi-select)

**Q12.** Would you be open to a chat about this? (Yes / No)

**Contact (optional, last page)**

**Q13.** How can we contact you for a chat? (name or nickname, plus one: email / phone / messaging handle) — *collected in the Form response sheet only.*

**Q14.** Roughly when are you free? (weekday daytime / weekday evening / weekend / flexible)

**Closing text:** "Thanks. We may not be able to invite everyone. If we invite you, we will send details of the chat and a consent form. You can ask us to delete your response at any time."

## Selection notes (internal; not in the form)

- Aim for mixed quotas, not the most enthusiastic: beginners and non-sewers (the main hypothesis group), some second-hand wearers, some who have used tailors. Add a few experienced sewers to learn what we are missing.
- Aim: about 10 customers and 5 tailors in the first weeks (proposed, not a requirement). Record the quota in the tracker.
- Do not recruit friends or family where avoidable; note it in the tracker if you do (`channel`).
- Assign participant codes when scheduling. Never put form responses in the repo.
