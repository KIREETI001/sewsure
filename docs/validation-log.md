# Validation log

What users, tailors and reviewers told us, and what we changed because of it. Maintained by L0 Lead Agent from D7 Research Ops session notes and founder reports.

## Rules

- **De-identified only.** Use participant codes: `P01`, `P02`… for users, `T01`… for tailors, `R01`… for sewist or supervisor reviewers. Never names, emails, phone numbers, photos or body measurements.
- Log only sessions run under an approved consent process (decision D12). If consent was not recorded, do not log the session.
- "What we heard" is a short paraphrase or a quote under 15 words. No full transcripts here; those stay in the consented research store.
- "What we changed" names the issue, PR or decision. If nothing changed, write "No change" and why. Keep negative results.
- "Link" points to the de-identified session note in `docs/research/`, or to an issue or PR.
