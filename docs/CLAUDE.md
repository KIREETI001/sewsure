# docs

Shared. Agents: X3 Report Writer (`reports/`, `adr/`), D7 Research Ops (`research/`), N6 Evidence Analyst (`evaluation/`), D8 Pitch & Demo (`pitch/`), L0 Lead Agent (digests and validation log).

## Rules

- State facts only from the repo, the evidence log, the validation log or the source PDFs. Cite which.
- Planned work is labelled planned. Proposed targets are labelled proposed.
- Every number has a source and a denominator. Keep negative results.
- Research notes are de-identified: participant codes (P01, T01), never names, emails or photos without recorded consent.
- FYP reports follow the NTU suggested format: Introduction, Work done so far, Future work, Conclusion. AI assistance is declared on the NTU GAI declaration form.
- X2 reviews anything that leaves the team (reports, pitch, website copy).
