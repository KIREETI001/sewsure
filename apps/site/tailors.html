For tailors and alteration shops

We are looking for a small number of tailors to try SewSure briefs in a pilot. The pilot is not running yet. Sign-ups are not open yet.

Everything on this page is planned. We have not set pilot terms, fees or a start date.

What a brief would contain Planned

  • The garment, with the photo and the change the customer chose.
  • The measurements the customer confirmed, each with its source and the date it was confirmed.
  • The redesign's checks: which passed, and which are not yet built and so were not tested.
  • A note that the preview is approximate and that the tailor should confirm the fit in person.

What we will not do Planned

  • Share a customer's details or photos with you unless the customer agrees.
  • List you publicly without your consent.
  • Tell customers that a tailor has approved a design, unless you have.

Help us get it right

We plan to invite tailors to talk with us about what a useful brief looks like for their work. Invitations will wait until our research consent process is approved, and nothing is booked yet.

Sign up as a tailor

We ask for your name, email, role and, if you like, your area. We will use them only to contact you about the pilot. The form is not connected to a backend yet, so nothing is sent.
