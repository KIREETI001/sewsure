Redesign a garment you already own

SewSure is a student project from NTU that is still being built. You photograph a garment, say what you want to change, confirm your measurements, and review redesigns that have passed our feasibility checks. Then you choose a DIY guide or a brief for a tailor.

What SewSure is for

Many people own clothes they no longer wear as they are: a shirt that is too long, a jacket with a shape they want to change, a skirt they would like to turn into something else. Changing a garment well needs a clear idea of what the garment can take, which measurements matter, and where the cut lines should go. SewSure aims to make those checks explicit before any fabric is cut.

How it works

The app walks you through four steps. See the full flow.

  1. Photograph the garment on a flat surface.
  2. Say what you want to change.
  3. Confirm the measurements the app uses.
  4. Review the redesigns that passed the checks, then take the DIY route or the tailor route.

Honest limits

Previews are approximate and are labelled that way. The app shows only the redesigns that passed its checks, and it may show fewer than three, or none, and say why. A check is only as good as the measurements and rules behind it, which are still being tested.
