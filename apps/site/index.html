Redesign a garment you already own

SewSure is a student project from NTU that is still being built. The planned app lets you photograph a garment, say what you want to change, confirm your measurements, and review redesigns that have passed our feasibility checks. Then you would choose a DIY guide or a brief for a tailor.

What SewSure is for

Many people own clothes they no longer wear as they are: a shirt that is too long, or a shirt with a shape they want to change. Changing a garment well needs a clear idea of what the garment can take, which measurements matter, and where the cut lines should go. SewSure aims to make those checks explicit before any fabric is cut.

How it works

Planned The planned app has five steps. See the full flow.

  1. Photograph the garment on a flat surface.
  2. Say what you want to change.
  3. Confirm the measurements the app uses.
  4. Review the redesigns that pass the checks.
  5. Take the DIY route or the tailor route.

Honest limits

The planned app will label previews as approximate. It will show only the redesigns that pass its checks, and it may show fewer than three, or none, and say why. A check is only as good as the measurements and rules behind it, which are still being tested.

The first checks are planned for oversized woven button-up shirts. Jersey tees may follow. For other garments, the planned app would show ideas only, clearly labelled, with no cut guide.

An AI model is planned to suggest redesigns from an approved list. The checks, not the model, decide whether a redesign passes.
