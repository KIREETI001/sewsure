About the team

SewSure is built by three students at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) as a final-year project. We are based in Singapore.

Who does what

  • Kireeti: platform, back end, cloud and releases.
  • Narthananilaa: garment rules, the feasibility checks and the evidence behind them.
  • Delfine: the mobile app, user research, this website and the pitch.

Why we are building it

We want people to get more use from clothes they already own, and we want tailors and home sewers to have a clearer starting point for a redesign. We are testing whether the checks and the guidance are useful before we make bigger claims.

Where we are

SewSure is a project in development. It is not yet publicly available. We plan to enter Imagine Cup 2027. We will update this page as the project moves on.
