Privacy

This page explains what we collect through this website and what happens to it. It is a draft for review and will change before any public release.

What the website collects

The website uses no tracking scripts and no advertising cookies. The waitlist and tailor sign-up forms ask for:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • your role; and
  • your area, if you choose to give it.

Please do not send photos of people or body measurements through these forms.

Where your details go

Right now, the forms do not send your details anywhere, and they do not save them. When a backend is connected, your details will be stored in a private store that the SewSure team controls, not in a public spreadsheet. We will not sell them. A hosting provider may store them on our behalf; we will name it here before sign-ups open.

Why we ask

We use your details only to contact you about the waitlist or a pilot you have asked to join. We do not sell them.

Keeping and deleting your details

We will keep waitlist details for Retention period to be set by the founders before launch. To ask us to delete your details at any time, email Contact address to be set by the founders before launch. We will confirm when they have been deleted.

Consent

When you join, you will be asked to agree that SewSure may contact you about the waitlist or a pilot. You can withdraw that consent at any time using the deletion route above.

Changes

We will update this page if what we collect or how we use it changes. The date at the bottom shows when it was last changed.

SewSure is an NTU final-year project. It is not yet available to the public.
