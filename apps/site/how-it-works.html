How it works

This page describes the flow we are building. Every step is planned or in development. None of it is available to use yet.

  1. Photograph the garment Planned

    Lay the garment flat and take a photo from above. The planned app will ask you to check the photo for blur, cropping and missing edges, and will flag low resolution.

  2. Say what you want to change Planned

    Choose a change, such as shortening a shirt. You can also ask for ideas. A change the app cannot check is shown as an idea only, with no cut guide.

  3. Confirm your measurements Planned

    Enter or confirm the measurements the redesign depends on. Each measurement records where it came from and when you confirmed it. If you change a measurement, the redesigns are checked again.

  4. Review checked redesigns Planned

    The planned app will show the redesigns that pass its feasibility checks, and what each check tested. It may show fewer than three redesigns, or none, and it will say why. An AI model is planned to suggest redesigns from an approved list. The checks, not the model, decide whether a redesign passes. Previews will be labelled approximate.

  5. Choose DIY or a tailor Planned

    DIY: you get a cut guide and step-by-step sewing instructions for the redesign you chose. Before cutting, you check the guide's measurements with a ruler.

    Tailor: you get a brief a tailor can read and respond to. You choose what is shared.

What the checks cover Planned

The planned checks are rules written for specific garments and specific changes. They would look at whether the garment has enough fabric and the right shape for the change. The first checks are planned for oversized woven button-up shirts. A redesign that fails a check will not be shown as a candidate. A redesign that passes will be shown with the checks it passed, and any checks not yet built will be named as such.
