How it works

This page describes the flow we are building. Every step is planned or in development. None of it is available to use yet.

  1. Photograph the garment Planned

    Lay the garment flat and take a photo from above. The app checks the photo for basic problems, such as blur, cropping and missing edges, before going on.

  2. Say what you want to change Planned

    Choose an intention, such as shortening, changing the neckline or turning the garment into a different piece. You can also ask for ideas.

  3. Confirm your measurements Planned

    Enter or confirm the measurements the redesign depends on. Each measurement records where it came from and when you confirmed it. If you change a measurement, the redesigns are checked again.

  4. Review checked redesigns Planned

    The app shows the redesigns that passed its feasibility checks. It shows what each check tested. It may show fewer than three redesigns, or none, and it tells you why. Previews are approximate and are labelled that way.

  5. Choose DIY or a tailor Planned

    DIY: you get a cut guide and step-by-step sewing instructions for the redesign you chose.

    Tailor: you get a brief a tailor can read and respond to. You choose what is shared.

What the checks cover

The checks are rules written for specific garments and specific changes. They look at whether the garment has enough fabric and the right shape for the change. A redesign that fails a check is not shown as a candidate. A redesign that passes is shown with the checks it passed, and the checks we have not yet built are named as such.
